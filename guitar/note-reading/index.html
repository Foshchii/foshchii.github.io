Microphone

While the microphone is on, an iPhone may play the app’s sounds quietly through the earpiece. This switch keeps them on the loudspeaker, but then the phone filters what the microphone hears, which can make notes harder to recognise. The sound is analysed on this device and never recorded or sent.

Tuner

Play one string at a time and let it ring. The needle shows whether it is flat (left) or sharp (right). Tap a string to hear its note.
