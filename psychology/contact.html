// contact

Write to me.

A few lines are enough: what’s going on, which language you’d like to speak, and whether you’d prefer online or in person. I’ll reply personally to suggest a time for a short first call.

02 — what to write

For example

“Hi Sviat, I moved to Copenhagen last year and I’m finding it harder than I expected. I’d like to talk in Ukrainian, online. Evenings work best for me.”

That’s all it takes. You can write in Ukrainian, Russian or English.
