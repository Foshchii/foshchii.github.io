Prefer to write?

A few lines are enough: what’s going on, which language you’d like to speak, and whether you’d prefer online or in person.

sviatoslav@foshchii.com

Email isn’t the most private channel, so keep the first message short.

“Hi Sviat, I moved to Copenhagen last year and I’m finding it harder than I expected. I’d like to talk in Ukrainian, online. Evenings work best for me.”

That’s all it takes. You can write in Ukrainian, Russian or English.
