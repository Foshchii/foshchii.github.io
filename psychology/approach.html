Approach

Calm, direct, and on your side.

Спокійно, прямо і на вашому боці.

I work with adults, one to one. A session is a conversation with structure: we start with what’s pressing, get clear on what you want to change, and work toward it at a pace that’s yours.

Principles

What you can expect from me.

Чого від мене очікувати

You set the direction

We work on what you came for. I’ll ask questions and say what I notice, but the goals are yours, and so is the pace.

Plain words

No jargon and no labels for the sake of labels. If something in our work isn’t helping, we say so and change it.

Between the lines

What we say and what we mean are often different things. Noticing that, carefully and without judging, is a large part of what I do.

Honest about limits

If what you need is beyond what I offer, I’ll tell you plainly and help you find the right place to go.

Confidentiality

What you say stays here.

Сказане залишається між нами

What you tell me stays between us. The only exceptions are the ones the law sets, such as a serious risk to your life or someone else’s, or a child at risk. If that ever comes up, I’ll talk to you about it first wherever I can.

I keep notes to a minimum and store them securely. I never use your story anywhere, not even anonymised, without your permission.

Limits

Knowing the limits.

Межі моєї роботи

Counselling isn’t medical treatment. I don’t diagnose, prescribe medication, or treat severe mental illness such as psychosis, severe depression, eating disorders or addiction.

If that’s what you’re facing, your GP (egen læge) is the right first step: they can refer you to a psychiatrist or an authorised psychologist. If you’re unsure which applies to you, write to me and I’ll help you work out where to go.

Questions

Common questions.

Часті запитання

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book or write to me directly. Sessions are private: they are not covered by the Danish public health insurance (sygesikringen).

Online or in person: which is better?

Both work well. Online sessions are by video from wherever you feel comfortable. In-person sessions are in Copenhagen. You can switch between them.

I’m not sure counselling is for me. Should I still get in touch?

Yes. That is what the first call is for: to talk it through, with no commitment.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on what you come with. Some people come for a few sessions about one specific thing; others prefer to work over a longer period. We review it together as we go, and you can stop at any time.

I don’t live in Denmark. Can we still work together?

Yes. Online sessions work from anywhere, as long as we can find a time that suits both time zones.
