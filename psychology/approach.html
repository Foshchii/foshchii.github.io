// approach

Calm, direct, and on your side.

I work with adults, one to one, online. A session is a conversation with structure: we start with what’s pressing, get clear on what you want to change, and work toward it at a pace that’s yours.

// principles

What you can expect from me

You set the direction

We work on what you came for. I’ll ask questions and say what I notice, but the goals are yours, and so is the pace.

Plain words

No jargon and no labels for the sake of labels. If something in our work isn’t helping, we say so and change it.

Between the lines

What we say and what we mean are often different things. Noticing that, carefully and without judging, is a large part of what I do.

Honest about limits

If what you need is beyond what I offer, I’ll tell you plainly and help you find the right place to go.

// areas

What people come with

Some examples of what we can work on. They’re starting points. You don’t have to fit one.

Crisis and starting over

  • war, displacement and the news from home
  • loss of people, of a home, of a life you’d planned
  • adapting to a new country, language and work culture
  • feeling stuck between “here” and “there”

Conflict and hard conversations

  • recurring arguments with a partner or family
  • conflict with a manager or colleagues
  • preparing for a conversation you’ve been avoiding
  • setting boundaries without guilt

Pressure at work

  • stress, overload and early signs of burnout
  • perfectionism and the need to be the best
  • career doubts and big decisions
  • rebuilding a career after a move

Family and generations

  • caring for ageing parents from a distance
  • raising children between two cultures
  • roles that change after a move or a new child
  • old family patterns that keep repeating

// confidentiality

What you say stays here

What you tell me stays between us. The only exceptions are the ones the law sets, such as a serious risk to your life or someone else’s, or a child at risk. If that ever comes up, I’ll talk to you about it first wherever I can.

I keep notes to a minimum and store them securely. I never use your story anywhere, not even anonymised, without your permission.

// limits

Knowing the limits

Counselling isn’t medical treatment. I don’t diagnose, prescribe medication, or treat severe mental illness such as psychosis, severe depression, eating disorders or addiction.

If that’s what you’re facing, your GP is the right first step: they can refer you to a psychiatrist or an authorised psychologist. If you’re unsure which applies to you, write to me and I’ll help you work out where to go.

// questions

Common questions

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book or write to me directly. Sessions are private: they are not covered by the Danish public health insurance (sygesikringen).

How do online sessions work?

We meet by video at the time we agreed. All you need is a quiet, private space and a stable connection; headphones help.

I’m not sure counselling is for me. Should I still get in touch?

Yes. That is what the first call is for: to talk it through, with no commitment.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on what you come with. Some people come for a few sessions about one specific thing; others prefer to work over a longer period. We review it together as we go, and you can stop at any time.

I don’t live in Denmark. Can we still work together?

Yes. Sessions are online, so you can join from anywhere, as long as we find a time that suits both time zones.
