How it works

Anonymous, read first, answered in the open

  1. Ask anonymously

    A nickname is enough. Leave out anything that could identify you or someone else, such as full names, workplaces or exact places.

  2. I read everything first

    Every question and comment waits for me before it appears. I may shorten a question to protect you, and I won’t publish everything.

  3. The answer is for everyone

    I answer here, so others with the same question can read it too. It’s general guidance, not therapy or a diagnosis.

Privacy

What happens to what you send

  • Your question or comment, and the nickname you choose, go to a Google Sheet in my Google account. Nothing else is collected: no email, no account, no cookies.
  • Only what I publish appears on this page. Posts I don’t publish are deleted within about a month.
  • I may shorten a post or take out details that could identify someone before publishing it.
  • Want something you posted taken down? Write to me at sviatoslav@foshchii.com with its title, and I’ll remove it.
